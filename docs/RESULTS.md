## Training exposure and lineage

The v4 run reached step 390. Selected `v4-mix-f` uses step-40 core/bridges/LoRA and the step-390 output adapter. V5 assembles that lineage and integrates state and runtime support. Packaging and runtime repair add zero training steps.

The inherited optimizer counter of 2,351 and the v4 run's 390-step counter belong to different stages. A downloaded inventory of 3,856,882 raw records describes available data, not the amount consumed by final training. The step-390 exposure audit recorded 88 distinct training addresses.

The records support runnable shared computation, selected task behavior, persistence and retrieval-grounded answers. General reasoning gains, mature decision competence, durable beneficial individual learning and discovery claims require corresponding broader evidence.
